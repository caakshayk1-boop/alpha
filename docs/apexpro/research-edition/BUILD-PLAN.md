# ApexPro Research Edition

Confirmed: ApexPro has screen/company/chart/market/macro data, published verdict/vet/ladder, a local Desk and visit snapshots. It has no positions, fills, costs or realised-P&L ledger. Tripwire's historical audit is not evidence about current ApexPro.

## Adaptation, all eight jobs
1. Glass Book → Research Trail: dated local captures and explicit actions; trace headline cohorts back to their rows. Never call observations positions.
2. Sit-Out Log → Evidence Gaps: held vet checks, missing statements, incomplete confidence, with supplied reasons and drill-through.
3. Honest Scoreboard → Evidence Census: Q/G/V/Tech distributions, median and measured denominator; select a bin to inspect constituents. No invented expectancy or realised returns.
4. Why this stock → Why this company: source-derived price context, financial facts, supplied verdict and evidence provenance, no speculative narratives.
5. Regime Weather → Market Anatomy: above-MA participation, trend cohorts and source breadth label with dates and exclusions; no new regime threshold.
6. Sector Pressure → sector treemap plus accessible sector return ladder, median 1M move and coverage, with filtered Discover links.
7. Watchlist Trips → existing price conditions plus recorded changes when the user actually observes them. No invented historical crossings or background notifications.
8. Ladder Share Cards → client-generated PNGs using published levels, basis, price date and educational watermark.

## Visual law
Keep the single token authority, three font roles and accessibility constraints. Replace the generic hero/card stack with a newspaper-like research masthead, a compact dial instrument, an interactive score field, ledger rows, ruled columns, an orange caution channel and labelled chart axes. Lime is navigation, cyan is evidence, mint/orange are signed data. Sharper primary surfaces, generous typography only where it earns hierarchy. All seven routes inherit a coherent editorial shell; Company gets anchored chapter navigation and a research spine, Compare a visual duel, Desk a chronological journal.
