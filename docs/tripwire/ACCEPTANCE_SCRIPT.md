# Five-user acceptance script

**Status: not run.** Automated tests are not user research. Nothing in this repository claims real-user validation. Run this with five people who invest or study markets in India, none of whom built Tripwire, and record the results below. Do not coach.

## Setup
- A recent iPhone (Safari) and an Android phone (Chrome); one person on a laptop. Fresh browser profile, so first use is real.
- 25 minutes each. Think aloud. Screen recording with consent. Say: "This is a research tool with paper money. You cannot lose anything here."

## Tasks (read one at a time; do not name screens or buttons)
1. "Tell me, in your own words, what this app is for." (after 20 seconds on the first screen)
2. "Find a company you already know. What would you want to know before buying it?"
3. "Open GCSL. Is this a company you would research further? Why, or why not?" (listen for: the counterargument, the evidence status, what was withheld)
4. "Write down why you are interested and what would prove you wrong. Choose a date to look again."
5. "How many shares could you afford to buy if you wanted to risk 0.5% of a ten-lakh account?" (planner; listen for the entry-price assumption)
6. "Someone says these picks are likely to win. What does the app say about that?" (the rank, the experimental label, the evidence)
7. "Close the app and open it again tomorrow. What do you expect to see?" (review date, what changed)
8. "Make a backup. Pretend you lost your phone. Restore it on this other device."

## Questions at the end
- What did you trust least? What did you trust most? Why?
- Was anything on a screen you did not understand? Point at it.
- Did any number look like a prediction? Which?
- What would make you open this every day? What would make you stop?
- Would you pay for it? What for? (ask, do not push)

## Pass criteria for a research beta (set before the sessions)
- At least 4 of 5 explain the product correctly in task 1, and none calls the picks recommendations or predictions.
- At least 4 of 5 complete tasks 4 and 8 without help.
- No participant reads "rank" as "probability" in task 6.
- No severity 4 issue open; no more than 3 severity 3 issues.
