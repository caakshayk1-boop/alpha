# alpha.askakshay.com: trading and research audit

Date: 15 Oct 2026 (MYT). Live build `5d1ad94` plus a docs commit (`2383b0a`). Read-only: no code edited during the audit.
Tools: Chrome via playwright-core 1.64.1, Lighthouse 13.5.0 (mobile, simulated slow 4G), axe-core 4.14.0 (WCAG 2.2 A/AA), curl.
Evidence labels: **Verified** = measured or reproduced this session. **Observed** = seen in a browser or file, not measured. **Inferred** = reasoned from evidence. **Not tested**.

## A. Executive summary
Alpha is a free, no-login research desk for Indian equities: a daily market read, a 989-company screen, company pages, five statistical "engines", IPOs, funds, news and weekly studies. It never executes trades. That boundary holds: there are no broker connections and no order flows (Verified: no auth, no payment or broker code in `src/` or `worker/`).

The data layer is honest by design. Every page carries a dated freshness stamp, missing figures say "not available" instead of being estimated, and engine results are shown against a random baseline after costs.

The weak spots:
- **Discoverability.** All 989 company pages are one URL to a search engine.
- **Legacy subdomains.** signal. and gems. still serve the old sites.
- **Hidden limits.** The engines' survivorship-bias note is hidden until an engine is opened.
- **No retention loop.** Nothing brings a user back beyond memory, since there's no account or email.

## C. Recommendation: **Conditional go** for a public alpha
Conditions: (1) retire or redirect signal. and gems.; (2) show engine limitations (survivorship, no slippage model, paper vs live) next to entry, stop and target levels; (3) add a one-line conflicts statement (whether the author holds positions); (4) add a CSP header.

## D. Top five risks
1. **High. Legacy subdomains serve old research under the brand.** signal.askakshay.com and gems.askakshay.com return 200 with the old sites ("Signal by Akshay Kothari…", "Gems — today's Indian market…"). `Sites/README.md` says signal. was scrapped and gems. 301s to Alpha. (Verified)
2. **High. The week-as-day breadth label is still live on news.askakshay.com.** `stock_screen.py` counts advancing and declining on `r1w`. On 13 Oct the week said 335/639 and the day was 641/327. Fixed on Alpha; the news-site fix is queued as a separate task. (Verified)
3. **High (growth). 989 company pages are invisible to search.** They share `/company/?s=…`, canonical `/company/` and the title "Company research · alpha", and the raw HTML has no company text. (Verified)
4. **Medium. Engine limitations sit away from the levels.** The survivorship paragraph renders only after an engine is opened (`engines/page.tsx:307`), and slippage is not mentioned anywhere. Meanwhile About claims the bias "is stated on the engines page". (Verified)
5. **Medium. The Company page is slow on phones.** Lighthouse mobile scores it 59 with 940 ms total blocking time, the worst route. (Verified, lab)

## G. Trading and research trust
- **Timestamps:** every page has a dataset stamp (session date, state) and each quote card shows its last trade (Verified). Times are in IST, with MYT used for Telegram (Observed). Currency is ₹ throughout; there's no US-priced row on Alpha's screen (Inferred from the screen being an NSE universe).
- **Facts vs models:** engines are labelled models, with "ranking skill" and "trading result" shown separately and a random baseline (Verified on /engines/). The AI explainer is off and says so (`503 not_configured`, Verified).
- **Backtests:** the sample is stated as "Prices through 14 Oct 2026 · 950 stocks", with a 0.25% round-trip cost, retrained each close, and the holdout reported (Verified). Survivorship is disclosed but hidden by default; slippage is absent (Verified). Live record since launch is shown separately from backtests (Observed).

## H. Desktop and mobile UX
The purpose is clear within five seconds: "A weak market, 13.8% below its high." plus four tasks (Observed). Search is everywhere (`/` and ⌘K). The phone bottom bar has five items. Tables scroll inside their own focusable region, not the page (Verified at 320–1440). The visual system is dark-first and dense; the approved v3 direction addresses this (see the design preview).

## I. SEO and accessibility
Every route has a unique title, a description of 80–174 characters, a canonical, an Open Graph title and exactly one h1 (Verified, rendered). robots.txt allows everything and points to a sitemap with 17 URLs (Verified). Company pages: see F3. Accessibility: axe is clean in both themes; tiny heat-map tiles are taken out of the tab order and every stock is listed as a link in the text version (Verified). Not tested: a real screen reader, 200% browser zoom on a device.

## J. Security and data integrity
- No secrets in `out/` (grep for key patterns: 0 files).
- No `target=_blank` without `noopener`.
- Two `dangerouslySetInnerHTML` uses, both static constants (theme script, early-fetch script, one CSS string).
- `/api/chart` rejects a path-traversal symbol (400).
- The explainer is same-origin, JSON-only, capped and rate-limited at 5/min per address through a Cloudflare binding (from the code and the 15 Oct commit history).
- When a feed fails, the summary returns 502 and both pages say so without estimating (Verified by forcing a 502).
- Missing: CSP (F7).
