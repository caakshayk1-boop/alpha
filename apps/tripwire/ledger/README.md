`ledger.json` is written by `.github/workflows/tripwire_ledger.yml` (nightly) and by nothing else. It is append-only: a night's picks are never edited, a night is recorded once, and an outcome is final once a trade closes or is skipped. Do not edit it by hand. It is the forward record the engines are judged on (docs/tripwire/ENGINES.md).

`book-snapshots.json` (18 Nov 2026) is the daily book snapshot: one row per session with the open book's R (the sum of each open position's recorded mark), closed R and counts, per engine. It is written by the same nightly job, only after the ledger assertions pass, and is write-once per day (src/booksnap.ts). It starts on 17 Nov 2026 (derived from the marks the ledger held that day); the days before it were never kept and cannot be rebuilt.
