# Rulebook revamp — 20 November 2026

Owner request: make it feel like an app, make everything live, restructure the pages so the SEBI wording comes last, and realign the fonts across the site.

## What changed
**Notice last.** The yellow "Educational and simulated" banners at the top of Today, Calls, Stocks and every stock page are gone. The full educational / SEBI notice is now the last block of every page (inside the footer, after the links and contact), and the "How to read this page" notes sit at the end of Today and Calls, after the content they explain. The header still carries the "Paper only" pill on desktop. The launch gate and `tests/launch.test.ts` now enforce the new rule (notice present, inside the footer, after `</main>`, no top banner) instead of the old one (banner above the fold).
*Decision for the owner:* this reverses audit item 1 (the notice where a decision could be made). Regulatory placement of a disclaimer is a legal question, not a design one; worth a securities-law opinion before the launch push (not legal advice).

**Fonts realigned onto one scale.** 35 ad-hoc sizes (10px to 40px, including several under 12px) are now six tokens (`--fs-xs` 12px to `--fs-2xl` 24px) plus two figure sizes and the documented display clamps, all in `tokens.css`. Nothing renders under 12px. Fira Sans carries everything that is read, JetBrains Mono everything that is counted. A stray undefined `var(--mono)` was fixed. `DESIGN.md` section 3 was stale (it named Fraunces, Inter and IBM Plex); it now states the real faces and the scale, and `tests/design.test.ts` fails if a size outside the scale appears.

**Live.** A status pill in the header of every page (and on a phone, where the terminal's old status was hidden) says what is true: "Live · 12s" (open, quotes arriving), "Delayed · 2m", "Waiting for quotes", or "NSE closed" / "NSE opens 09:15"; it needs no network of its own (IST clock, calendar and the age of the last quote). Today's Score leaders gained Last and Day columns that follow live quotes. On Stocks the first 25 rows follow live quotes (price and 1D) and say so; rows beyond 25 stay closes. Quotes stay delayed and unofficial (Yahoo via the Worker): that is a data-licence limit, not something a page can fix.

**App feel.** `viewport-fit=cover` (the bottom bar's safe-area padding did nothing without it), the iOS and Android standalone meta tags, a manifest with an id, scope, display override and three shortcuts (Today, Calls, Stocks), and a small service worker that serves an offline page when the network fails. The worker never serves pages or data from a cache, so no stale figure can be passed off as current. It is registered 8 s after load because registering at load took away the first screener-to-stock view transition.

**Layout.** Today now leads with The book and the Daily brief, then the Market pulse (the widget menu follows the same order).
