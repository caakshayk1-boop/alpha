# ApexPro
<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform
web

## Purpose and users
Independent Indian equity research with market context, company evidence and a private Desk. The owner has delegated a full design and tooling rebuild and deployment. Primary-user assumption: an individual researcher comparing NSE companies and revisiting saved notes.

## Capabilities
Source-labelled fundamental/technical scores with confidence, source vet and ladder, historical price/RSI charts, divergence, Time Machine, comparisons, watchlist, notes, review dates, checkpoints, backup and evidence exports. The canvas supports sector/horizon exploration, explicit research lenses and filtered CSV export.

## Constraints
Vanilla JavaScript ES modules. Existing endpoints/scoring/vet/ladder read-only. No fabricated missing values, returns or activity. Educational language. Preserve localStorage keys and Desk data. Self-host all scripts/fonts, maintain CSP. Keyboard, reduced motion and colourblind modes remain first-class.

## Product principle
Move from broad market context to a company, its countercase, and a dated personal observation without inventing certainty.
