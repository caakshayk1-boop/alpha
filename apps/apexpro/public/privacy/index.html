Privacy

Short version: ApexPro has no accounts, no sign-in, no advertising and no third-party scripts. What you add to My Desk stays in your own browser.

What stays on your device

Your watchlist, notes, conviction ratings and settings are written to your browser's local storage on this device. They are not sent to ApexPro. Clearing the site's data in your browser removes them, and they do not follow you to another device.

What the service sees

ApexPro runs on Cloudflare Workers. Like any web host, the service receives your IP address, browser type and the page or data request you make, and Cloudflare keeps request logs for operating and securing the service. Company and market data requests pass through the ApexPro Worker to public data sources and are cached briefly; they carry no personal information from you.

Cookies and tracking

ApexPro sets no cookies and loads no analytics, advertising or social scripts.
